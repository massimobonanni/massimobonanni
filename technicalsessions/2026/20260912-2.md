# The Foundry Forge: Crafting AI Solutions from Azure to the Edge
##  12/09/2026 - AI Industrial Summit 2026 - Sofia
### Abstract 
Organisations adopting AI solutions often face strategic decisions about where workloads should run, how to meet governance requirements, and how to balance innovation with operational constraints. This session introduces Microsoft Foundry on Azure and Foundry Local as two complementary approaches for delivering modern applications. It explains what each platform offers, the types of business challenges they address, and how to align deployment choices with regulatory, performance, and cost considerations. Using real-world scenarios, the session demonstrates how cloud and local models can coexist to support digital transformation, accelerate delivery, and enable responsible adoption of AI technologies.
